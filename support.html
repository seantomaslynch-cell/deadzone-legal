?Common questions

Is my progress saved?

Yes — your outpost, roster, and progress are saved automatically to your device. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what's stored and how backups work.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Every ad in Deadzone is optional and skippable, and nothing in the game is ever locked behind one — ads only ever add a bonus on top of what you can already earn for free.

I found a bug, or the game crashed. What should I include?

Email temveno@gmail.com with:

  • Your device model and OS version (e.g. "iPhone 15, iOS 18.2")
  • What you were doing right before it happened
  • A screenshot, if you have one

How do I request a refund?

Deadzone currently sells nothing in-app, so there is nothing to refund. If that changes in a future update, refunds for App Store purchases are handled directly by Apple through your purchase history.

How do I delete my data?

Open the game's Settings menu and choose Erase Save, or simply uninstall the app — either one removes everything immediately from that device. Nothing is stored on a server.
